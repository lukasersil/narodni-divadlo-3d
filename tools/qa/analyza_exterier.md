# Analýza exteriéru – srovnání modelu s podklady (3. 10. 2026)

Zprávu napsal pomocný agent. Porovnal pravoúhlé rendery modelu s fotkami v `03_exterier_reference`, Fialkovými plány 1883 a řezem a pohledy z roku 1914. Původní znění je anglicky a je níže. **Čísla jsou návrhy k ověření.**

Jednotky jsou metry. v je souřadnice v rámci budovy; na západní fasádě zhruba platí v ≈ vzdálenost od SZ nároží − 1,8. Výšky jsou nad ulicí, ±0,5 m, pokud není uvedeno jinak.

## Original report (English)

### 1. West (river) façade, main block

The façade is symmetric about v≈28.6. Sequence: pylon | 3 rectangular bays | 5 arched bays | 3 rectangular bays | connector.

The model has 12 identical arched windows on the main floor (piano nobile), at 4.1 m pitch, with half-column frames and alternating pediments. The bay count is right; the window types and positions are wrong.

**Main floor** (floor about 8.6 m; top of the order about 16.6 m):
- **NW pylon (v −1.8…4.8):** one arched window, 1.5×3.0, sill 9.7, at v≈1.6, with a small window above. Model roughly right (v 1.2).
- **North side section (risalit):** projects about 1.0 m forward of the centre (plan 04). Three bays at v 7.3 / 11.1 / 14.7, pitch about 3.7, Corinthian pilasters between. Each bay is a double window:
  - a tall rectangular window 1.4×3.0 (9.6–12.6);
  - a straight hood on consoles at 13.2–13.7;
  - a small window 1.2×0.8 (14.4–15.2) in an eared frame with a small broken pediment.
- **Centre, set back:** 5 arched windows at v 19.2 / 23.9 / 28.6 / 33.4 / 38.2, pitch 4.75.
  - Engaged Corinthian half-columns, Ø≈0.9, at v 16.8 / 21.5 / 26.2 / 31.0 / 35.8 / 40.6; shafts start about 9.0, capital tops about 16.6.
  - Openings 3.2 wide: sill 9.5, transom/springing 13.7, crown 15.3.
  - Central mullion; festoons in the spandrels; no pediments.
- **South side section (risalit):** v 42.9 / 46.5 / 50.3, same as the north section.
- **Connector bay (v≈55):** a large arched window 2.7 wide, 9.5–14.9, above an arched portal 2.2 wide with its crown at about 7.0.
- **Balcony:** a continuous balustraded balcony at about 9.0–9.9 from the porch terrace to v≈52. The model only has it on the porch.

**Frieze (16.6–20.3):** one oval window per bay (13 including the pylon and connector), 1.25×0.85, centre about 18.3. The model's ovals sit about 1 m low (centre ≈17.2). The ovals are flanked by carved acanthus scroll panels.

**Ground floor** (band rustication, 0–8.4): each bay has three small windows stacked vertically, about 0.8 m wide:
- basement window at 1.1–1.6;
- ground-floor window at 3.8–5.0;
- mezzanine window at 5.7–7.0.

The model has one 1.6×2.6 window per bay.

**Porch (podjezd):** v 4.8–17.0 (model runs to 18.3), 6.3 m deep, roof terrace at about 8.6.
- 3 arched openings, 2.4 wide, crown ≈7.5, at v 7.3 / 11.1 / 14.7. Glazed, with dark-red wooden doors and mascaron keystones.
- One drive-through arch about 3 m wide in each end face.
- The model has only 2 arched windows here.

**Actors' door (v≈46.5):** 1.2×4.2 with a hood at ≈4.4. Myslbek's reclining Opera/Drama group sits above it at 4.5–6.0.

**Statues on the balustrade:**
- Wagner's groups: Zpěvohra centred at v≈11, Činohra at v≈47. The model puts the north group at v≈22.
- Single statues at v≈5, 16.5, 41 and 53.

### 2. North façade

**Loggia:**
- 5 bays at **4.15 m** (plan 04), not the model's 4.94.
- Single columns at c±2.1 and c±6.2 (c = middle of the front); paired columns on the corner piers at c±10.4 (±0.5).
- Loggia floor ≈6.6. The balustrade between the column pedestals tops out at ≈7.7. Capital tops ≈15.0.
- Frieze with 5 relief panels (acanthus with animals) at 15.0–17.6; deep cornice up to ≈21.

**Ground floor under the loggia:** 5 **open** arches, span ≈2.8, crown ≈5.0, with mascaron keystones. They lead into a vaulted porch ≈4 m deep, with steps up to the doors at the back. The model has flat dark doors flush with the façade.

**Pylon north faces:**
- Ground floor: a doorway ≈1.6×3.3 with a triangular pediment (top ≈5.3). The model has a plain window.
- Niche with statue at 9.1–12.3: correct.
- A small square window at ≈13.9–15.2 is missing.
- The frieze oval should sit at ≈18.3.
- The relief panel on the pylon attic is correct. There are **no corner urns**; remove them.

**Attic north face:** 5 windows (the model has 8), plus 3 doors at terrace level (≈21.5–24). The terrace and pylon tops are stone paving, not copper.

### 3. East façade, main block

**11 bays at ≈4.0 m (v≈8–52):**

- Straight hood at ≈12.4; small window 1.3×0.9 at ≈13.4–14.3.

**Ground floor:** an open **arcade gallery** ≈2.3 m deep runs the full length and continues past the connector.
- 11 semicircular arches, span ≈3.5, springing ≈4.3, crown ≈6.0.
- Slender columns Ø≈0.4, on a balustraded plinth ≈1.3 m high.
- The gallery roof is a balcony with a balustrade at ≈7.5–8.5.
- The model has 8 dark arched doors set into the wall instead.

**Attic:** single windows at ≈3.9 m pitch (v≈15.6–27 and ≈43–51), plus a projecting box with 4 windows at v≈31–42 whose cornice is ≈1.5 m below the attic cornice. The model has 14 uniform windows. Lower confidence.

### 5. Roof and skylight

**Dome:** the slate runs from 30.6 to a top edge at ≈39.3; the model's 39.0 is fine.

**Crown:**
- Gilded fascia 39.3–40.6, with mouldings top and bottom.
- Gilded cresting 40.6–41.8: rosette medallions between posts, spear finials up to ≈42.4.
- Four corner spires: gilt ball Ø≈0.75 at ≈43.6, spike tip at ≈46.8.
- The windowed box in the 1914 section is the **skylight lantern**, set ≈2.4 m inboard (38.9–41.3) and mostly hidden; a long copper/glass ridge ≈3×26 m.

**Dormers:** 12 in total, 4 per long side and 2 per short side.
- West at v≈15.0 / 24.1 / 33.2 / 42.3; the east side mirrors this.
- North/south pairs at u≈−5.2 and +3.2. Window centres at ≈35 m.
- Each is an oval window ≈0.8×1.1 in a gilded frame, with gilded side scrolls and a gilded palmette on top; dark bronze body; patinated copper hood.

**Attic storey:**
- West: 9 windows at 4.55 m pitch, centred on v 28.6; eared frames, openings ≈1.6×1.1, centre ≈26.5; fluted pilaster strips.
- North: 5 windows. East: see §3.
- **Balustrade on the attic cornice (≈30.6–31.6) all the way round**, with gilded tripod vases ≈1.3 m tall on the 4 corner pedestals.

**Main roof** (between the main balustrade and the attic): on the west, east and south, a dark **slate sloping strip** from ≈21.6 to ≈23.5 against the attic wall. The north side is a stone terrace.

**Connector:** a flat copper deck at ≈21.6 behind balustrades on both façades. **Delete the hip roof.**

**Provisional block:**
- An attic storey **flush with the façades**, 21.4–26.8.
  - West: 5 windows — a small cartouche-framed window in each corner bay and 3 larger windows (1.4×1.6) in the centre.
  - East: windows in eared frames.
- Cornice and balustrade up to ≈28.5, with obelisks at the corner bays and urns.
- Copper hip roof, eaves ≈28, ridge ≈31, with a row of 4–5 skylights.
- No dormers, no chimneys.

**Schulz house:** **no roof storey.**
- Balustrade directly on the main cornice at 21.9–23.5.
- Urns at ≈24.4, two obelisks up to ≈26.7.
- Low copper hip roof, ridge ≈25.3, one hatch.

**Chimneys:** none visible anywhere in the photos.

**Small cupola with lantern** (ext_detail_kupolka_lucerna.jpg): **not part of the theatre**. It is one of the two toll-house pavilions at the Most Legií bridgehead in front of the NW corner.

### 6. Materials and colours

- **Dome "stars":** pale grey/white slate crosses (≈5 light diamond slates, ≈1 m across) on a diagonal grid over blue-grey slate (#4F5868). **Not gilded.**
- **Main roof strip:** slate, not copper.
- **Dormers:** patinated copper hood, dark bronze body (#3A3530), gilded trim.
- **Crown fascia and cresting:** gold leaf (#E0B850, roughness ≈0.3).
- **South wing roofs:** verdigris #7FA595 (the model's is too pale and minty).
- **Window frames:** dark brown (#3B3530); the model's glazing bars are too red.
- **East gallery columns:** a lighter grey stone than the façade.
